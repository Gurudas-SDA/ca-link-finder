kiṁ vā keśava bhāṣaṇena bahunā puṇyair yaśo labhyate ||106||		By Gemini 2.5 Pro
'On earth, a certain very small mountain was playfully lifted by your arms; for that, you are perpetually called the lifter of Govardhana in heaven and on earth. I carry you, the bearer of the three worlds, on the tips of my breasts, so that is not considered a great feat. O Keshava, what is the use of many words? Fame is obtained through merit.' ||106||

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Rādhikā is saying to Kṛṣṇa. She's embracing Him, and saying, "Oh Kṛṣṇa, You have lifted some mountain. Not so heavy. With Your strong arms. Well, that mountain was not so heavy. But still, all over the three worlds, everyone is glorifying You for this act. You have become so famous. What a heroic act. I am not glorified. I am always carrying two mountains. And not only that, but (..) You are, though You are the You uplift the whole world, I am uplifting You and holding You. On my mountains." Kiṃ vā keśava vāsanena bahunā(5). "But I don't get any credit for this. So I have to say there's one universal truth." Puṇyair yaśo labhyate(5). Who becomes famous? It is not dependent on talent. Sometimes, a person not very talented becomes world-famous and someone who is very talented, no one ever heard of them. So, puṇyair yaśo labhyate, Rādhikā said, "It's a true thing that fame only comes from your puṇya, your pious activities in the previous life." So the dhvani is that, "Oh, You have become so famous because You are so pious. Even though what I have done is more heroic than You, but I am so impious. What can I do? This is why You are famous and what I my activities don't count." And in this way, Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are laughing and joking with each other in the kuñja. "When will I be there massaging the lotus feet of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa and overhearing Their beautiful jalpa, Their conversation?" This is the result, yathā yathā gaura-padāravinde(2), of taking shelter of Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Rādhā-padāmbhoja-sudhāmbu-rāśiḥ(2), the ocean of the nectar of serving Rādhikā's lotus feet rises in the heart. Gaura-premānande! Haribol! (..)
