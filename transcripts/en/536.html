So then Śrī Kṛṣṇa, He was so overjoyed, "Oh, She forgave Me." But Kṛṣṇa could not say anything, Rādhikā could not say anything. They have to hide Their feelings, this is called pracchanna-kāmukatā. Because They have to hide Their feelings, then this feeling become more powerful. So this is called parakīya-rasa. It was no one heard of such love before Caitanya Mahāprabhu, and Caitanya Mahāprabhu came to distribute the realization of these pastimes and the service of Rādhārāṇī through nāma-saṅkīrtana. Nitai Gaura Premānande! Haribol!
