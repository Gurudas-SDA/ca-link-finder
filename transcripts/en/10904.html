caitanya-rasa-vigrahaḥ

’bhinnatvān nāma-nāminoḥ		Because there is no difference between nāma (Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s holy names) and nāmī (Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the Person who possesses the names), the holy name is a transcendental wish-fulfilling gem (cintāmaṇi) and the very embodiment of the transcendental mellows of divine consciousness (caitanya-rasa-svarūpa). Thus it is completely pure and eternally liberated.
