Just as the elements enter into the creation, and also remain outside. So similarly, oh, outside is Kṛṣṇa's prakata-līlā and inside is aprakata-līlā. This is the prema-tattva of catuḥ-ślokī Bhāgavatam. And so if you cannot understand, it is my inability to express these things. But who can express? These things are... Mahāprabhu said, in Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja in Caitanya-caritāmṛta, he said, "I want to tell the līlā of Caitanya Mahāprabhu, but actually it should not be spoken, because if anyone would tell this truth, everyone will think that he's mad." But a madman anyway has no discrimination and cannot stop himself from speaking, so here we go, Caitanya-caritāmṛta. Gaura Premānande! Haribol! We'll sing one kīrtana. Hari-kathā is going away, so we have to sing kīrtana for hari-kathā, and pray that hari-kathā will come back again tomorrow.
