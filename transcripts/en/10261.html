maṅgalāni samīhase		Durvāsā Muni said: My dear King, today I have experienced the greatness of devotees of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, for although I have committed an offense, you have prayed for my good fortune.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Today I have seen the glories of a Vaiṣṇava." Durvāsā said, "I tried to kill him, but he was praying for my benefit." So, nowadays this Kali-yuga has progressed and this vision is lacking in many Vaiṣṇavas. A person thinks, "If this devotee, he served my guru so much, but today just recently he's not becoming favorable towards me, so I will not support him anymore, even though I have many faults myself, but there's no need to serve him." No, this mood is wrong. This is a blockage, this is anartha. This is not the sign of guru-bhakti, actually. So, in this way, (..) I'm offering my śraddhā-puṣpāñjali at the lotus feet of Parama Pūjyapāda Śrīla Bhakti Dayita Mādhava Gosvāmī who is, even today, (..) it was his disappearance day yesterday, but even today the light of his glories is illuminating us and teaching us what is really the nature of guru-bhakti. Śrī Śrī Rādhā Śyāmasundarajīu kī jaya.

(Kīrtana)
