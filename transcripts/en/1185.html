And then one can enter into the highest possibility, the greatest prospect of any soul, that is rādhā-dāsyam, the eternal service of the divine couple, Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. Rūpa Mañjarī, Rati Mañjarī, the maidservants of Rādhikā, they are qualified to be Her sakhīs. More, they're qualified to themselves be yūtheśvarīs, to be group leaders with their own groups of sakhīs even. Yet by humbly accepting the position of maidservant of Rādhikā, they have the good fortune of being able, at that time when Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa meet, and the friends of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, Lalitā, Viśākhā, Citrā, Campakalatā, they'll have to recede to a distant place, those maidservants can stay and enter the kuñja. And Śrīla Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī wrote in Muktā-carita how Rūpa Mañjarī, she sacrifices, she has no desire to meet with Kṛṣṇa, she has no desire to be a yūtheśvarī, no desire to be a sakhī, because if she were to take that position, she would not be able to witness and hear the astonishing pillow talk of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, their loving prattle which is (..) very confidential and secret. So those who take shelter of Nityānanda Prabhu, gradually by His mercy, they'll get the shelter of Rūpa Gosvāmī and have the opportunity to serve in those most confidential pastimes.
