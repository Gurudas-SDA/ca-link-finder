So Rādhikā, she's so sharp, when Kṛṣṇa says, "Uttiṣṭha tātarau," the word 'tarau' can mean in a boat, but it can mean in a taru, in a tree. So Rādhikā said, "What relation do I have with the sun?" Kṛṣṇa said, "No, no, nau-prasaṅge." "Prasaṅga" means the subject, and "nau" means a boat, so "nau-prasaṅge", "I was talking on the subject of a boat." But "nau" can be—mean the dual pronoun in Sanskrit, first person dual possessive pronoun; it means that which belongs to two of us, our. And "saṅga" means association, and "prasaṅga" means very, very close association. So Rādhikā took Kṛṣṇa saying "nau-prasaṅge" on the subject matter of a boat, she took that to mean as if Kṛṣṇa was saying, "I was talking about our very intimate association."
