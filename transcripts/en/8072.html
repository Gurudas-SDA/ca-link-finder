So because the sakhīs always keep Me in their eyes and they cover their eyes with kājal, and the kājal is black, this is why I am black." Kṛṣṇa Kanhaiyā Lālā kī jaya! Jaya Jaya Śrī Rādhe! Gaura-premānande hari hari bol!
