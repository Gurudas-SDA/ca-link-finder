With closed eyes they are simply listening in silence to the sweet vibrations of His flute, and they are not attracted by any other sound. Surely these birds are on the same level as great sages.

one gopī said, "These birds of Vraja, they are great ṛṣis." They're going into the high branches, that means to the highest branches of the Vedic knowledge. Instead of being restless they sit with half-closed eyes. Vigatānya-vācaḥ, means when they hear Kṛṣṇa's flute, they give up speaking. Or anya-vācaḥ means words other than those words describing the love of Kṛṣṇa. All these birds they're all ṛṣis speaking hari-kathā. They cannot stop.
