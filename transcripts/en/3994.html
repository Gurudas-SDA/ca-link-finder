Sri Prem Prayojan Prabhu: Meaning of Jaya Dhvani - Sri Prem Prayojan - 2019-03-07

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[00:00:09] Do you know what this is called? When we say "jaya jaya" to all these personalities at the end of a kīrtana? What is it called? Jaya-dhvani. It's called the jaya-dhvani. It is really prema-dhvani. That means, just like when you hit a bell, then the strike of the bell, but then after that there are waves of sound coming. So that is called the dhvani. So when the kīrtana stops, that was the striking of the gong. Then in the hearts of all the devotees, then the waves of love start to flow. And the prema-dhvani comes, "Oh Gurudeva, oh Parama-gurudeva, oh Parameṣṭhi-gurudeva, oh Navadvīpa-dhāma, Jagannātha Purī-dhāma, Vṛndāvana-dhāma, Govinda, Gopīnātha, Madana-mohana!" So never think that anything we do is some mechanical or dry ritual. I hope that at the end of this program you are feeling some of the prema-dhvani, the echoes of love.
