Bhagavad-gītā As It Is by A. C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda

───────────────────────── [25]

ye yathā māṁ prapadyante
tāṁs tathaiva bhajāmy aham
mama vartmānuvartante
manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ		As all surrender unto Me, I reward them accordingly. Everyone follows My path in all respects, O son of Pṛthā.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Kṛṣṇa said, "As someone approaches me, I reciprocate with them accordingly." So you may be rich, you may be poor, you may be educated, you may be doing yoga, you may be doing austerities, doesn't mean anything. The only thing which is viable is how we are devoted to the service of Supreme Lord. And He reciprocates and becomes devoted to His devotee.[02:08:44] So Lord Rāma, He gave the blessing to Kākabhūṣaṇḍi, "Time will have no power over you. May you adore me unceasingly." And in this way, Kākabhūṣaṇḍi revealed this own heart and his own life history to Garuḍa. So how merciful, how kind is the Supreme Lord that He removed the pride of Garuḍa. And arranged that the king of birds would have to bow down to the crow. And how beautiful is a sādhu-saṅga. Garuḍa is thinking it was the this situation is the blessing of Lord Rāma upon me that I can meet with you. And Kākabhūṣaṇḍi is thinking, you are not really bewildered. It is a blessing upon me that I can see you, have your association. And by this past time, it has been shown that our moha, our bhrama, our bewilderment, (..) it can only be removed by sādhu-saṅga, association with saints and hearing and chanting in their company.[02:10:56] So Śrī Jagadānanda Paṇḍita said,

(Prema-vivarta, 6.13 by Śrīla Jagadānanda Paṇḍit)

Śrī Śrī Prema-vivarta by Śrīla Jagadānanda Paṇḍit. Śrī Chaitanya Sāraswat Maṭh

───────────────────────── [26]

sādhu-sange kṛṣṇa-nāma—ei mātra chãi
saṁsāra jinite āra kona vastu nāi		I want only to chant the Name of Krsna in the association of the sādhus—there is no other way to conquer saṁsāra.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Oh, I only want sādhu-saṅga, the association of the saints, and Harināma-saṅkīrtana. Ei mātra cāi, this is all I desire. Who told me? Jagadānanda Paṇḍita. Saṁsāra jiniti āra kona vastu nāi(25). In the matter of conquering over the illusion of this saṁsāra, the endless chain of birth and death, there is no other vastu. Nothing else can save us.[02:11:56] Gaura-premānande hari hari bol!
