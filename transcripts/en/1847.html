So something of the original spirit of the Six Gosvāmīs and Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas here in Vṛndāvana due to these atrocities, something was lost. Many of teachings became misunderstood. And the glories of loud hari-nāma-saṅkīrtana and dancing with all Vaiṣṇavas also became somewhat covered.
