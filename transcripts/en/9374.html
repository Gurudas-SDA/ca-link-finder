So love is never expressed, it's always hidden, but it shows the in an indirect way. If love is directly expressed, then it will go down, it will be cheapened. Just like one man, he had a friend, and the friend came to his house, and the friend saw, "Oh, he's cooking samosas and a spinach and kheer, my favorite preparations." So then he asked, "Oh, are you cooking for me?" His friend said, "No, I'm cooking for me." And then he cooked and offered, and then very silently he put them on the table before his friend. Then his friend said, "Oh, I know that he knows these are my favorite things and he cooked them for me." And his heart is melting and he's eating, and the two of them inside, they relish so much love, but without words, no words. But if he said, "Did you cook this for me?" Then he said, "Yes, I spent so many hours yesterday going to the supermarket, and the ingredients were so expensive, and I've been up since early this morning cook," then the love will go down. But when the love is not expressed, when it's hidden, then it's relished heart to heart. So in the same way, when Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa meet, then they never tell, "I love you. Yes, I also love you." They say, "Oh, what are you doing here? You are a thief, you have to pay a tax!" "I'll not pay a tax! If the air that touches you touches my ghee, then it will be ruined!" So this is what, all prema-kalaha, the kathā of love. And by this quarrel, then only they are arranging for their abhisāra, exactly where they will meet, when they will meet, (..) for the next pastime the next day.

[00:23:30] So then the next day, Kṛṣṇa was waiting here, and Rādhikā and Her sakhīs came here, and Śrī Kṛṣṇa took the tax (..) from Śrīmatī Rādhikā. That means He played with Her in the kuñjas here. But Rādhārāṇī took the tax back, why? Because the prema of Rādhikā was so high that in Their loving pastimes, Śrī Kṛṣṇa fainted. So She took the tax of Kṛṣṇa's citta. So the place where Rādhārāṇī took the tax back by making Kṛṣṇa faint in the ecstasy of prema, that is called Dāna-nivartana-kuṇḍa. So here is Dāna-ghāṭī, where Kṛṣṇa demanded the tax, and here is where Rādhikā took everything from Kṛṣṇa. She took His consciousness, made Him faint, so really Kṛṣṇa became anaṅga, an-aṅga, unconscious of His own aṅga, of His own body, here in Dāna-nivartana-kuṇḍa. So Caitanya Mahāprabhu was the first person to manifest the greatness of the anurāga of Śrīmatī Rādhikā, and He empowered this realization in the heart of Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī. And he has expressed it in his Dāna-keli-kaumudī, Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī in his Dāna-keli-cintāmaṇi, this is the great treasure of the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas. This is why it is said,
