Śoce tato vimukha-cetasa indriyārtha, that in this world there are thousands of people, and they are all in ignorance. And they're working (..) very hard for some illusory happiness. They are like slaves carrying a heavy burden of the burden that they made for themselves. I am very worried about them. Prāyeṇa devā munayo sva-vimukti-kāmāḥ, there are many sages who go to the forest and they live alone, and they're trying to get their own liberation. But I am not like them. I don't want to go to the spiritual world and leave everyone here. So I am begging You, please, take all the karma of all the living entities in the world and give it to me, and let me suffer, and let everyone else be liberated." Lord Nṛsiṁhadeva said, "Oh, Prahlāda, you have defeated Me. I cannot do this. I cannot make you suffer. But I will give you this benediction, that if anyone will listen to this conversation between us, then very soon they'll develop pure prema, pure love, and go to the supreme..." Prahlāda Mahārāja kī jaya! Lord Nṛsiṁhadeva kī jaya! So by hearing this kathā, this discussion about pure devotion, you should know that it's extremely powerful, it will have a very strong effect and change your life completely. Very soon you may discover that you are in Vṛndāvana. And in sādhu-saṅga experiencing the nectar of pure bhakti.

[01:20:24] Then Lord Brahma came. And he offered a prayer. He said, "Oh, my Supreme Lord, who can understand Your divine activities? It was You who inspired the Four Kumaras to go to the gate of Vaikuṇṭha. It was You who inspired Jaya and Vijaya to stop them. It was You who inspired the Kumaras to become angry and give a curse. So it was You who made Jaya and Vijaya become Hiraṇyakaśipu and Hiraṇyākṣa. Oh, my Lord, it was You who inspired me to give the benedictions to Hiraṇyakaśipu that he becomes so powerful. And at the same time, You made Prahlāda Mahārāja take birth as the son of that same demon. It was You who inspired Hiraṇyakaśipu to try to kill his son, and then it was You who appeared and saved him. Oh, my Lord, everything is Your līlā. We cannot understand You, only we bow down at Your lotus feet and chant Your holy names."
