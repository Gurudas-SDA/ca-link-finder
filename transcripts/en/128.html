Sri Prem Prayojan Prabhu: Sri Prem Prayojan - What is Culture

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[00:00:06] What is culture? In the ancient tradition of the Vedas, it is described that the pure mind is by nature full of joy, full of happiness, and peaceful. But because our mind has become contaminated by the gross physical elements which enter into the mind through the activities of sense gratification, so then the mind, which is by nature very light, very expansive, becomes narrow, it contracts, it becomes darkened. So that is called vikṛti, the distortion of the mind. The original nature of the mind is called prakṛti, that is its nature, prakṛti nature. And the distortion, the contraction, the darkening of the consciousness is called vikṛti. So there's a way of living, there's a way of experiencing the arts, the way of experiencing the sciences, the way of experiencing religion which gradually reverses the distortion of our mind, which causes the contracted mind to expand, which causes the darkened mind to become filled with light, which causes the (..) sorrowful mind to become full of joy. So that method of reversing the distortion is called saṁskṛti. So prakṛti is the nature, natural condition of the mind, vikṛti is its darkening and contracting, and culture is the saṁskṛti, the reversal bringing us back to a pristine, pure state of blissfulness, of joy, of broad-mindedness, of open-heartedness, and deep peace.
