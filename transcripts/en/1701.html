jaya jaya rādhā-kṛṣṇa yugala-milana
ārati karoye lalitādi sakhī-gaṇa		All glories, all glories to the meeting of the Divine Couple, Śrī Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa. The sakhīs, led by Lalitā-devī, perform Their ārati ceremony.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

In the meantime, Subala was doing housework. This is called sakhya-rasa. He was separated from Kṛṣṇa, but he was feeling the happiness that he had served his sakha. Gaura-premānande hari hari bol! Jaya! So give your praṇāms here, and with kīrtana, we're going to the Ratnavedī.
