Just as the moon appears from the ocean, so Śrī Kṛṣṇa, imbued with the sentiment and complexion of Rādhikā, (..) manifested from the womb of Śacī-mātā here beneath this neem tree. So they called Him; His nickname is Nimāi. And His grandfather, maternal grandfather, Nīlāmbara Cakravartī, gave Him the name Viśvambhara, which means who maintains and nourishes the whole universe. That is, who nourishes everyone with prema. Jagannātha Miśra kī jaya! Śacī Maiyā kī jaya! Baby Viśvambhara Miśra kī jaya! Nitāi Gaura premānande Hari Haribol!
