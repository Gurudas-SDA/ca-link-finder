For example, a person who lives in poverty and then they have the same mundane life every day, they can also they can feel some happiness. But if someone then gives them a life which is astonishing and full of great festiv-, exciting festivals one after another, that is not the same as simply existing. Similarly, if you remember someone but you don't love that person, that remembrance may give some happiness but not real joy. So Krishna's saying so similarly, the devotees and including Himself, they always feel separation and some the joy of separation from the ones they love. But if someone else comes along and causes them to remember in a very special way and increase that separation, then, oh, this is (..) the supreme ānanda.
