So, when Vijaya-kumāra and Vrajanātha heard this passage in Jagadānanda's Prema-vivarta from the mouth of Śrīla Raghunātha Dāsa Bābājī Mahārāja, their hearts became agitated and they plunged into in the ocean of mahābhāva expressed by Jagadānanda Paṇḍita. Bābājī Mahārāja was practically unconscious after reciting these verses. Vijaya-kumāra and Vrajanātha, they were feeling ecstasy, but Bābājī Mahārāja was practically, his consciousness was out of this world for a long time. And then when he came out into external consciousness, he embraced Vijaya-kumāra and Vrajanātha, and he wept. And at that time, Bābājī Mahārāja sang this song:

This kīrtana is extraordinarily ecstatic essence of Nāma-bhajana and the subjective experience of a sādhaka as he goes through the stages of Nāma-bhajana, from the initial stage where he feels complete dry, and no taste, to the stage where Nāma begins to manifest. And then slowly takes over all his consciousness and mind, and then gradually reveals to him the form of Kṛṣṇa, and Vṛndāvana, and then his own svarūpa and how he participates in the pastimes. All explained in this kīrtana. That kīrtana we will discuss tomorrow. Hare Krishna.
