──────────────────────────────────────────────────

As Śrīla Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī, being fully absorbed in his siddha-deha, his identity as Rati Mañjarī, “Oh, may I always serve the lotus-eyed Śrīmatī Rādhikā. May I always remember Her sweet smile. May I always glorify Her heart which is melting with compassion. I have no other object of service.” So this Rādhā-dāsyam, the service of Rādhikā, this is bhargo devasya dhīmahi, the purport of all the Vedas.
