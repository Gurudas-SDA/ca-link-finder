At first I saw that You are a sannyāsī, but now I am seeing a dark complexion cowherd boy and just near to that dark complexion cowherd boy, I see kāñcana-puttalikā, a golden doll, and the effulgence of that golden doll is spreading and covering the Śyāma complexion." That is Gaura, that is Rasarāja-mahābhāva. Rasarāja, rasika-śekhara Śrī Kṛṣṇa in completely bathed in the golden complexion of mahābhāva-svarūpiṇī Śrīmatī Rādhikā. That is Gaurāṅga, like this. So we can also, all these various permutations and combinations of the revelation and conceal- concealment of Yogamāyā are also at work in Gaura-līlā. Gaura-premānande! Haribol!
