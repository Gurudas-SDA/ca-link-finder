[00:00:09] Advaita Ācārya is sitting in his home weeping, “Hā Gaura, hā Gaurāṅga.” Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura is sitting alone in his home weeping, “Hā Gaura.” Murāri Gupta is sitting alone in his home weeping, “Hā Gaura.” Kholāvecā Śrīdhara is sitting in his broken hut weeping, “Hā Gaura.” (..) All are drowning in the ocean of separation.
