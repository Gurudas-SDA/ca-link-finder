[00:51:42] (Kirtan) So, in this way, the kathā of the abhiṣeka that was previously very short was expanded by Madhukaṇṭha for four hours. And all of the very intimate sakhīs and sakhās, they sat listening without moving, like statues. Then, just as they had all come together secretly for Hari-kathā, then they all disbanded and returned to their homes.
