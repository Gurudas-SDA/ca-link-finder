Oh, where is the son of Nanda Mahārāja, the hero of the rasa dance, who plays so gently upon His flute, whose body is effulgent like a blue sapphire? Without Him, I am dying from this disease of separation. Oh, sakhi, please bring me the medicine. What medicine? The son of Nanda Mahārāja, He is the only medicine. I know that those who break dharma and do irreligious things, what happens to them? Then the reaction comes to them, their fate is that they must suffer. Dhik vidhi, (..) I curse you. Why? Because I have no chance to serve Krishna. I can suffer (..) million times over for Krishna, no problem. But without me, perhaps His heart is breaking.
