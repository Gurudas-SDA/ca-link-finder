It's the nature of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra, if you'll do japa of this mantra, then bhāva will come, very ecstatic love for Kṛṣṇa will appear. So that's what happened. He was chanting harināma and Nāma Prabhu manifested His svarūpa, His beautiful form. Mahāprabhu became mad. He saw Kṛṣṇa playing flute beneath a kadamba tree in Vṛndāvana, was smiling and looking at Him. And His fragrance was so attractive, at once Mahāprabhu wanted to embrace Him. But when He tried to embrace Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa disappeared. And Mahāprabhu fell to the ground unconscious. You cannot tell for how long He was unconscious. But when He, (..) came back to sense again, He was crying, sometimes laughing, sometimes dancing, sometimes running here and there. He thought, "What happened to me?" Before, He was a paṇḍita, Nimāi Paṇḍita, very absorbed in vyākaraṇa, grammar, and Navya-nyāya, logic. But now it seemed that all of His knowledge has been covered over. He was worried, so He came back to His Gurudeva.
