[00:00:01] When I used to come here with my Gurudeva, then at this place there was no car park, this building did not exist. This was a forest of kadamba trees. So this area, it is called Kadamba-khaṇḍī. There are several Kadamba-khaṇḍīs in Braj, but this is the Kadamba-khaṇḍī of Girirāja Govardhana. So, you may know that in Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, in Daśama-skandha, chapter 29 to 33, this is the Rāsa-pañcādhyāyī, five chapters describing the rāsa-līlā. This rāsa-līlā is the śāradīya-rāsa, autumn rāsa-līlā in Vṛndāvana. But in the Gīta-govinda of Jayadeva Gosvāmī, there is described the vāsantika-rāsa, the springtime rāsa-līlā of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. And so this that took place here in this forest of kadamba trees, this Kadamba-khaṇḍī. So this is the specialty of this place, that in the rāsa-līlā in Vṛndāvana, Kṛṣṇa disappeared from the rāsa and everyone was crying and looking for Him. But here, in the vāsantika-rāsa, Rādhārāṇī disappeared from the rāsa-līlā, and Kṛṣṇa was crying and looking for Her. So we prefer this līlā, actually. Rādhikā should not cry. Kṛṣṇa can cry, but not our Rādhikā. So this place is so glorious, it is the place of the vāsantika-rāsa-līlā. Kadamba-khaṇḍī kī! Jaya!
