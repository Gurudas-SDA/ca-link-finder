Although Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one in Their identity, They separated Themselves eternally. Now these two transcendental identities have again united, in the form of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. I bow down to Him, who has manifested Himself with the sentiment and complexion of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī although He is Kṛṣṇa Himself.

And He had to hear Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam from He wanted to realize rādhā-bhāva, so He had to hear the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam spoken by the person who had the realization of rādhā-bhāva. Who has realization of rādhā-bhāva? Śrīmatī Rādhikā Herself, and therefore everyday Sacīnandana Gaurahari, he used to go and hear Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam from Rādhikā Herself in the form of Śrī Gadādhara Paṇḍita. And thereby, even Kṛṣṇa Himself has shown, na buddhyā na ca ṭīkayā(4), even Kṛṣṇa could not know by His intelligence, even Kṛṣṇa could not know by the ṭīkās, He could only know by hearing from the person with anubhāva. So in this way, I offer my śraddhā-puṣpāñjali millions of times to Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, Sacīnandana Gaurahari, Śrī Śrī Kiśora-Kiśorījīu, and all the assembled Vaiṣṇavas,
