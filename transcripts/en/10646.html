Yahāṁ gopiyoṁ kā prema sarva-śreṣṭha hai. Jo bhī ācārya āye, unhoṁne sab ne gopiyoṁ ko guru mān ke hī prema se Ṭhākur-jī kā dhyāna kiyā, smaraṇa kiyā, sevā kī. Aur Ṭhākur-jī yahāṁ sab ke āge-pīche phirte haiṁ. To āp logoṁ ko bhī jaba prema se gopiyoṁ ke bhāva se darśana karne ke liye Braja meṁ jāoge, to kahīṁ na kahīṁ āp ko svānubhāva sab ko jatāyeṁ, aisī maiṁ Ṭhākur-jī se prārthanā kartā hūṁ.

[00:00:58] So, Mahārāja-jī is saying, "I am praying to Śrīnātha-jī that He will give blessings to all of you, and that lālasā, that greediness with which you came to Braja-maṇḍala, that you should have that greediness to serve the Ṭhākur-jī following the mood of Braja-gopīs." All of our ācāryas have said the highest bhāva, the highest loving feeling of all, is there in the gopīs of Vṛndāvana. So, he is giving the blessing to all of you that may you engage in hearing, chanting, remembering, and meditating, fully absorbed, following the mood, loving mood, of the gopīs of Vṛndāvana.
