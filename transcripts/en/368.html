He didn't want to be separated from Mahāprabhu because Mahāprabhu had given him so much mercy from the beginning of His stay. And later, when his parents, (..) (..) were old, and they passed away, he went to Vṛndāvana, and under the guidance of Rūpa and Sanātana, he became Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī. His whole life, he never saw Mahāprabhu again. And he was chanting and weeping, "Oh, when I was a boy, Mahāprabhu stayed in my house. I used to go to the river with Him carrying His kamaṇḍalu and dry dhotī. And He used to tell me the sweet pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. And now by His mercy, I am in Vṛndāvana with Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana."

[01:25:03] Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī, he collected from śāstra all kinds of pramāṇa to, (..) show the teachings of Caitanya Mahāprabhu. And on the basis of his notebooks, oh, Jīva Gosvāmī wrote all the Sandarbhas, but all of this is from Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī's notebooks. And Śrī Sanātana Gosvāmī compiled Śrī Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, all of this was also from the notes of Gopāla Bhaṭṭa. And Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī, he came in Vṛndāvana, and he was worshipping Śālagrāma-śilā. And in his intense mood, because of his intense mood of separation and eagerness to serve, this śilā manifested spontaneously the form Śrī Rādhā-Ramaṇa. And this beautiful vigraha of Rādhā-Ramaṇa is non-different from Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Mahāprabhu appeared in this form to pacify the separation of Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī.
