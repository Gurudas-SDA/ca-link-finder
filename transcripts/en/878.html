Lord Brahmā prayed, "Oh, Kṛṣṇa! It would be a matter of great fortune for me if in some future life I could take birth here in Vṛndāvana as a stone. I am not qualified to be like Yaśodā-maiyā, I am not qualified to be like Subāla, I am not qualified even to be like these cows. If Brahmā, the greatest devatā in the universe, he said, 'I would be lucky if I could be a stone in Vṛndāvana outside the house of one sweeper lady.' So after during the day she has been sweeping the drains and she comes home from work and she's not clean, before she goes in the house she has to wipe her foot on one stone. Brahmājī said, though he's Brahmā, the highest of all the brāhmaṇas, he said, 'I will be lucky if the sweeper lady of Vraja will wipe the dust from her feet on my head.' Tad bhūri-bhāgyam iha janma kim apy aṭavyām(3). Why? Because yaj-jīvitaṁ tu bhagavān nikhilaṁ mukundaḥ(3). Because for every Vrajavāsī, Kṛṣṇa is their life. At every moment, twenty-four hours a day they only think of Kṛṣṇa. How to serve Kṛṣṇa and please Kṛṣṇa, they don't know anything else. (..) If they pray to Lord Nārāyaṇa, they only pray to Lord Nārāyaṇa, 'Please, let Kṛṣṇa be happy.' So Brahmājī said, 'If the Vrajavāsīs who have such love, I can be touched by the dust of their feet, then perhaps all my ignorance, my foolishness... No, all my jñāna, all my knowledge will go away, and my heart can be full of prema, full of love like them.' Because they always remember the feet of Kṛṣṇa. And the Vedas themselves, Brahmājī said, 'I am a follower of the Vedas, śruti-mṛgyam eva, but those Vedas that I am following, they themselves are searching for the dust of the feet of Kṛṣṇa.' So then when Brahmājī was praying like this, Kṛṣṇa, He was not looking at him. Brahmā realized, 'I don't belong here.' So he gave praṇāma, and he got onto the back of his swan, and Brahmājī began to fly away back to Brahmaloka. And as he was flying, he looked back. And when he looked back, he saw Kṛṣṇa's friends were coming and running there. 'Oh, Gopāla! Gopāla, what were you doing? Who is that person you are talking to? Cal ma, cal ma! Who is that person we saw with four heads? What was he saying to you? Bla-bla-bla-bla-bla-bla-bla! We cannot understand.' When Kṛṣṇa saw His friends pretending to be Brahmā and playing like this, then Kṛṣṇa was laughing. He was very happy. And Brahmājī was looking back. He said, 'I offered so many prayers in Sanskrit, He was not listening. His friends are only going, "Bla-bla-bla-bla-bla," and Kṛṣṇa is very happy.' How wonderful is Vṛndāvana! And then Brahmājī, he returned to Brahmaloka. So we are all very fortunate that Bhagavān, He is the ocean of rasa, He is the ocean of love, and He is (..) very merciful.
