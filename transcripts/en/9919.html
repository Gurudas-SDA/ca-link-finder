Do you know this verse, Bhagavad-gītā, also chapter five? Kṛṣṇa is saying, “Na kartṛtvam.” That means the living entity does not initiate activities. So you wake up in the morning, “What will I do today? Okay, I’m going to decide, I will go to the park.” So then you have the subjective experience, “I just decided what to do.” So Kṛṣṇa said, “No, na kartṛtvam,” no, you didn’t. You did not decide. You did not initiate anything. “Na karmāṇi.” Then after deciding, “I went to the park, I did it.” Kṛṣṇa said, “No, na karmāṇi,” you did not initiate the activity and you did not do the activity. “Lokasya sṛjati prabhuḥ.” This has multiple meanings, but just to stay on the subject of your question, “Na karma-phala-saṁyogam.” And when you are working and you get the result of your work, let’s say you’re doing some job and at the end then you get paid, and then with this money you buy a car. “So I have the now arranged for the fruit of my work, this car.” No, you didn’t. Because maybe the company you’re working for went out of business and in the end of the week they could not pay you. You cannot manage that. Or maybe you got the money and on the way to buy the car someone stole it from you. You’re not in control. So you don’t initiate the activity, you don’t perform the activity, and you don’t create the result of the activity. How is it going on? “Svabhāvas tu pravartate.” It’s going on by svabhāva.

[00:30:08] So svabhāva means saṁskāra. By the saṁskāras in your citta, if they’re in the subconscious mind, and when a saṁskāra from the sub-conscious mind comes into the conscious mind, you identify with that, and then that initiates the activity. If in your previous life you were smoking a cigarette, then in this life that saṁskāra will come into your mind and say, “I want to smoke a cigarette.” Actually, the saṁskāras are just like pictures, they cannot force you to do anything. But if you’re identifying with them, then you just go along with it, so that’s why I said autopilot. The common person, because they identify with their mind, is just on autopilot, and there’s a flow of saṁskāras from the subconscious mind to the conscious mind, and whatever however those saṁskāras are moving, they’re just going on. And out of illusion they think, “I am choosing, I have free will, and I am doing everything of my own volition.” You have free will, but once you surrender to māyā and become covered, that was your free will, and now the material energy is doing everything. So saṁskāra means the impressions in your citta.

[00:31:40] Now one could say, “Well, the impressions in my citta cannot bring the result of my work, because that involves the whole universe.” If your boss is going to pay you or a thief is going to steal your money, like that, that was not the impression in your citta. It is the impression in someone else’s citta, or it can be in the cosmic (..) universe. And so how your fortune unfolds, svabhāvas tu pravartate, everything is going on by svabhāva. So svabhāva refers to both types of impressions, the ones in your own citta and the ones in the cosmic citta, the mahat-tattva, as well. So if you do the karma-mukha-sukṛti, then it makes an impression in the cosmic citta. And so you can get good luck. The fruit of your work is coming. But that is you are not aware of that yourself. So the impressions in the universe which bring about your good fortune, this is sukṛti. And the saṁskāras in your own citta which affect your state of mind, it’s also a kind of saṁskāra. But that is your subjective experience. So the subjective saṁskāras, that is saṁskāra. And the sukṛti is a kind of saṁskāra, but it’s related to your destiny of your life. So it’s not limited to only what’s in your consciousness, but in the (..) universe by which (..) your fruits of work are coming, and your destiny is unfolding. Understand? So both are types of saṁskāra. But when we make the distinction between sukṛti and saṁskāra, we mean the (..) sukṛti is in the cosmic citta by which your good fortune comes about, it may be the jñāna-mukha-sukṛti, karma-mukha-sukṛti, bhakty-unmukhī-sukṛti.
