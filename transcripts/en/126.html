Sri Prem Prayojan Prabhu: Sri Prem Prayojan - The Absolute Reality

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[00:00:07] Welcome to Caitanya Academy. When a general person tries to think of what is God, then they think of the greatest thing they can imagine, a being who must be omnipotent, omnipresent, and the omniscient, all-knowing, all-pervading, and all-powerful. So this is a good start, but the Vedas tell us that reality, the personality of reality, has much more to offer than this. In practical life, we see that people are rushing to the theatre, rushing to the cinema, and in previous times rushing to the library to take a book which will absorb them, to draw them into an adventure in which they will go on a journey filled with a variety of emotions, with the entire spectrum of feelings, with the entire palette of suspense, compassion, romance, adventure, artistry. Where do these qualities come from? So the Vedas say:

raso vai saḥ, rasaṁ hy evāyaṁ labdhvānandī bhavatiOnly when one achieves Kṛṣṇa, the embodiment of pleasure and the ocean of rasa, does one actually become transcendentally blissful. *

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Supreme Personality of Godhead is Himself the embodiment of the entire spectrum of all the flavours of love, of all the flavours of drama for which our heart is hankering. The artist who has painted the beauty of the skies, the mountains, and the oceans in this material world, which is inherently of the nature of dull, insentient, and dark matter, that very person has manifested a realm which is filled with unlimited perfection of all the aesthetic flavours of love, adventure, emotion. That is the Parabrahma, the supreme reality. And He is known in Sanskrit by the name Kṛṣṇa.
