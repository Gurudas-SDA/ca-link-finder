In our spiritual life, there are many important tattvas with which we must familiarize ourselves and gradually realize, such as jīva-tattva, the reality of the nature of the soul; māyā-tattva, the reality of the nature of the illusory material world; Kṛṣṇa-tattva, the nature of Svayaṁ Bhagavān, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Śrī Kṛṣṇacandra, Vrajendranandana, Śyāmasundara; bhakti-tattva, līlā-tattva, rasa-tattva—so many tattvas. But of all the various tattvas, the first and foremost tattva is guru-tattva, the principle of Śrī Guru. This is most important because if one doesn't understand guru-tattva, the principle of Guru, then one will never come to realize all the other tattvas. And conversely, if a person will understand and realize śrī-guru-tattva, the principle of the spiritual master, then the door is open to realize all the other profound tattvas.
