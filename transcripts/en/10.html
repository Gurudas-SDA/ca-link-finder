Please be merciful and tell us about it (..) in detail." Bābājī Mahārāja said, "There is a very deep analysis of the ten offences to the name in the Padma Purāṇa." Now Bābājī Mahārāja is going to explain each one in detail. So we will continue tomorrow. Śrī Harī-nāma-saṅkīrtana kī jaya!
