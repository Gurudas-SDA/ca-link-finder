And then by continuing to hear the hari-kathā from such exalted persons, then you'll get āsakti, and then bhāva, so at every stage.” You know, sometimes, “Oh, I already heard, I was initiated by Prabhupāda in 19 whatever, 100 years ago.” No, at every stage, even in bhāva, you have to hear and associate with sādhu. This Śrīla Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura says in the commentary on that verse.
