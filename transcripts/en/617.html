So, then, Paramahamsa Premadasa Bābājī, he said, "O Ananta dasa, Bābājī Mahashaya is also fully qualified to answer such a question. So, today we will all listen to his instructions." So, then Ananta dasa, he said, "As I receive your order, then I must certainly say whatever I know. I should begin by remembering the lotus feet of my Gurudeva." Who was that? Sri Pradyumna Brahmachari. This kuñja was Pradyumna Brahmachari's kuñja, Pradyumna-kuñja. "So, by remembering my Gurudeva, Sri Pradyumna Brahmachari, who is an intimate associate of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu..." Then, after offering praṇāma to his Gurudeva and all the assembled Vaiṣṇavas, he began the explanation of saṁsāra, of material existence. And what he said we will hear tomorrow. Śrī Gaura-premānande! Jai!
