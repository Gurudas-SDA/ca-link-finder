Śrī Jayadeva Gosvāmī has written, "keśava dhṛta-haladhara-rūpa."(2) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Himself has appeared as the holder of the plow, Haladhara Balarāma. So Balarāma Prabhu is Śrī Kṛṣṇa's first expansion.

[00:02:05] There are so many different types of expansions. The first type of expansion is called prakāśa. There are two types: prabhāva-prakāśa and vaibhāva-prakāśa. So when Śrī Kṛṣṇa expands Himself, and His expansion has same mood and same complexion, that is called prabhāva-prakāśa. For example, during rāsa-līlā Kṛṣṇa expanded to dance with all gopīs, these are called the prabhāva-prakāśa expansions of svayaṁ-rūpa, His original form. So in the rāsa-līlā svayaṁ-rūpa, the original form of Kṛṣṇa, was dancing in the middle with Rādhārāṇī, and around the outside, His prabhāva-prakāśa expansions were dancing with vraja-gopīs. But if Kṛṣṇa Himself expands Himself having a different complexion and a different mood, then this is called vaibhāva-prakāśa. So Kṛṣṇa has sevya-abhimāna, that means the self-conception that "I am to be served." But when He expands Himself with a sevaka-abhimāna, "I am the servant," and His complexion is different, instead of being śyāma, He's fair-colored. So that vaibhāva-prakāśa, which has different mood and different complexion, but is Kṛṣṇa Himself, is called Baladeva Prabhu.

[00:04:45] So Baladeva Prabhu is so eager to serve Kṛṣṇa, He serves Him in all ways and in all rasas. Baladeva is the adhiṣṭhātṛ-devatā of sandhinī-śakti, existence. He has become the whole dhāma. And He becomes all the paraphernalia for the service of Kṛṣṇa: āsana, vasana, bhūṣaṇa, pādukā, chatra, cāmara. That means Kṛṣṇa's shoes, Kṛṣṇa's bed, Kṛṣṇa's clothes, Kṛṣṇa's ornaments, His cāmara fan, His the umbrella, Balarāma has become all paraphernalia. He became mṛdaṅga to serve Kṛṣṇa.
