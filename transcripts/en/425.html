Tribhuvana-maṅgala-kārī Śrī Hari-nāma-saṅkīrtana kī jaya! Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Govinda, Gopīnātha, Madana-mohana jīu kī jaya! Ananta-koṭi Vaiṣṇava-vṛnda kī jaya! Samāgata bhakta-vṛnda kī jaya! Nitai Gaura-premānande Hari Hari bol! Jaya Jaya Śrī Rādhe!
