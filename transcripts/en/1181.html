Hearing this, Jagadānanda Paṇḍita laughed. What is that? It means, one madman, he's telling another madman that the whole world has become mad. Bāulake kahio loka haela bāula. Bāulake kahio hāṭe nā bikāya cāula. One madman says that there's no longer demand for rice in the marketplace. So, Jagadānanda just thought, okay, it's funny. I'll deliver the message to Mahāprabhu. So from Navadvīpa he traveled, he came to Purī and he met with Mahāprabhu. Mahāprabhu was sitting among his associates. Jagadānanda Paṇḍita said, "Oh, Advaita Ācārya sends this message to you. Bāulake kahio loka haela bāula. Bāulake kahio hāṭe nā bikāya cāula." The devotees laughed hearing this funny riddle. But Caitanya Mahāprabhu didn't laugh, he became very serious. And Svarūpa Dāmodara also became very serious. No one understood what the meaning was. Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī understood because his heart is like, he is Caitanya Mahāprabhu's abhinnātmā. He's the second body of Caitanya Mahāprabhu. So he understood, but in humility, he acted as if he didn't understand. He said to Mahāprabhu, "What does it mean?" Caitanya Mahāprabhu said, "I don't know. Advaita Ācārya is such a mystical Brahmin that he can compose riddles that even I cannot understand."
