When a person wandering throughout the universes becomes eligible for liberation from material existence, he gets an opportunity to associate with devotees. When he associates with devotees, his attraction for You is awakened. You are the Supreme Personality of Godhead, the highest goal of the supreme devotees and the Lord of the universe.'

—"O infallible Lord, the jīva has been wandering in this world of birth and death since time without beginning. And when the time comes for him to leave this cycle of life and death, he associates with Your śuddha-bhaktas. From the moment he receives this association, his mind becomes firmly fixed on You, who are the soul and supreme shelter of the surrendered devotees. You are the controller of all and the cause of all causes."

[00:57:05] So Bābājī Mahārāja said, "Since time without beginning, the soul, who is eternally bound by māyā, has been moving lifetime after lifetime, taking birth again and again according to his karma. Sometimes he becomes a deva, a god or goddess. Sometimes he becomes an animal. From time to time, he may attain the human form of life. But from the moment he attains the association of a sādhu, on the strength of past sukṛti, then he fixes his mind very firmly on Śrī Kṛṣṇa, and hence he attains rati."
