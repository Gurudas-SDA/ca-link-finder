So because His heart is under control of love of His friends and the love of gopīs, He's lying to His: "I'm going to I have to take bath in Yamunā, it's Ekādaśī."

[01:07:28] (..) Mother Yaśodā heard this, she turned to one of her friends and she said, "(..), O sakhī, some enemy must have come in the house. Who told my son he has to follow Ekādaśī? Was it you?" She's very upset. Because she wants to feed Him sweet rice and very delicious things. "Who told my son about Ekādaśī?" All the Vedas say: "Follow dharma, you must follow Ekādaśī." And mother Yaśodā, because she's think, "If my son doesn't eat grains, He'll not be healthy. His body is so soft, why should He do austerities?" So mother Yaśodā is asking her friends, "Was it you? Who told Him? What enemy came here and told my son about Ekādaśī?"

[01:08:39] So then mother Yaśodā turns to, "Oh Kṛṣṇa! Why do You want to go to the Yamunā? You know what's there? Oh, under the where the leaves fell from the trees and they're all fallen leaves on the bank of Yamunā, Bakāsura and so many demons are hiding, waiting to jump out! Don't go there, my son. I have already prepared a nice warm bath for You and very fragrant oil. Why go to so much trouble to go to take be relax and take bath at home."

[01:09:26] So this is Vṛndāvana. When the love comes, it's so sweet and beautiful, it goes beyond dharma. Sevā—sevā means to serve, to please—it goes beyond dharma. Just like in the Vedas say: it is a dharma, it is a dharma for the wife to serve her husband. Veda does not say that it's dharma that wife should scratch her husband or pull his hair or bite him or anything. But anyway, what is more pleasing? So sevā is superior to dharma.

[01:10:12] So in the same way, this is why Nārada Muni chastised Vyāsadeva: "jugupsitaṁ dharma-kṛte 'nuśāsataḥ(1)"—"Why did you speak about this bogus dharma?" Because like the bumblebee, you should give up the dharma, give up the mada from the elephant, and fly to Vanamālī Kṛṣṇa and serve Him just to please Him. Pleasing Kṛṣṇa is the highest dharma:
