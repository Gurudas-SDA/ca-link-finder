Because if you have ghee and it's (..) very hot, and it's extremely hot, little smoke is rising from it, so can you cool it down by taking a drop of water? What will happen if you put a drop of water in boiling ghee? (..) Like this. So meeting with Kṛṣṇa, but only seeing Him for a moment without being able to fulfill in even a small way their ambitions to serve Him and love Him in His presence, so that association for just a day or so will be like only a drop of water in the boiling ghee of their separation. So to bring Him and then take Him away suddenly, that would be worse than not bringing Him at all. And if I will tell, 'O Nanda and Yaśodā, don't worry, Kṛṣṇa will surely return in four or five years when He's solved all the geopolitical situation with all the demons and armies everywhere going against the Yadu dynasty, it will just take Him four or five years to solve this, and then He'll come back,' then they'll think, 'Four or five years?' and they will also die. And if I tell, 'It's okay,' if I lie and say He's coming in four or five days, then after four or five days they see that He's not here, they will know that I was lying, and they'll also become hopeless. So what can I say?" So what Uddhava, he came up with a solution for this. But he will tell Nanda Mahārāja tomorrow. Gaura Premānande Hari Haribol!
