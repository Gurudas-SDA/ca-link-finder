Then the next part of the gopāla-mantra relates to the līlā-svarūpa, the nature of Krishna's pastimes. So one must hear about līlā-tattva from pure vaiṣṇavas. Then the next one is called the parikara-svarūpa-jñāna. One must hear about the nature of Krishna's associates. (..) And then the next one is the called vallabha-svarūpa-jñāna. That means how those associates, they have render loving service to Krishna. Then the next one is called sva-svarūpa-jñāna, knowledge of one's own spiritual form. And then the next one is the cit-prakṛti, that means what is the nature of your own services. So when we hear the kathā of these the six svarūpas, then this nourishes our sambandha-jñāna and then the mantra begins to manifest and one experiences the (..) mantra-siddhi.
