Quoted by Śrī Prem Prayojana Prabhu

───────────────────────── [11]

mananāt trāyate yasmāt tasmān mantraḥ prakīrtitaḥ |That by meditating upon which one is delivered is called a mantra [lit., ‘a means (tra) of meditation (man)’].

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

By which a person is liberated from the (..) captivity of the mental constructs, that is called mantra. So kīrtanīyaḥ sadā hariḥ(4), one should continuously chant, that means one should always remain in that divine state of awareness of Śrī Kṛṣṇa beyond the limitations of the vikalpa mental constructs.

Devotees who are not in that liberated state, their whole talk among themselves, the questions and the answers, all in the state of ajña-rūḍhi-vṛtti. Therefore, and they're satisfied with that. They think, "Oh yes, we have had a very elaborate discussion of philosophy today." So Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura said, "If you want auspiciousness in your life, then it is essential, ignoring the opinions of millions and millions of conditioned souls, one should submit himself only to hearing the divine message from a transcendental source."
