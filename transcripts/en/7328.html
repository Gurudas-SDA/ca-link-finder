'eka' aṅga sādhe, keha sādhe 'bahu' aṅga
'niṣṭhā' haile upajaya premera taraṅga		"When one is firmly fixed in devotional service, whether he executes one or many processes of devotional service, the waves of love of Godhead will awaken.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Some persons practice only one aṅga, one limb of bhakti, and some practice many limbs of bhakti, but all of them, as soon as they have niṣṭhā, stabilization in their practice, then the they experience waves of love rising in the heart.

[01:51:16] Another question? Yes. Что душа должна сделать со своей стороны, чтобы ей посчастливилось встретить истинного гуру? "What soul should do from its perspective, from its side, that it could make increase possibility (..) to meet pure guru?" (..) Yes. Only our Prabhupāda Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura said, when a person realizes that everything around them is in a state of decay, then he quoted from Psalm 6 of the Bible, he said, "Then you should and one should water his couch with tears," that means weep very profusely for the God's mercy. Then Supreme Lord, being very kind, will appear in front of him, not in His Vṛndāvana-svarūpa, but in the guru-svarūpa, the form of the spiritual master.
