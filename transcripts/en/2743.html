Today, all the sufferings of the residents of Godruma-dvīpa went far away. And Gaurāṅga has caused the appearance of the marketplace of the holy name. Wandering all over the land of Nadia, Caitanya Mahāprabhu and Nityānanda Prabhu came to this haṭṭa, this marketplace of the holy name. And Nityānanda Prabhu, He was dancing like a wrestler. (..) Yes, that means in the Vedic culture, when the wrestlers, before they fight, they slap their arms and their legs like this. In this way, all the devotees headed by Advaita Ācārya, they were dancing from one ghāṭa, one bathing place, to another along the Gaṅgā. And Kali Mahārāja, the personality of Kali-yuga, seeing them, he became terrified. Because where there is harināma-saṅkīrtana, he cannot exert any influence. Oh, what happiness all the jīvas, all the living entities felt today, seeing Caitanya Mahāprabhu dancing. And hearing the sound of the kīrtana, even the atheists, their hearts are broken.
