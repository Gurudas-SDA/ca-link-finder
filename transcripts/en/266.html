Secondly, the Supreme Absolute Truth, does He have form or not? And thirdly, what is the genuine, actual nature of pure bhakti, transcendental devotion?

[00:01:22] First of all, we should know what is sad-guru. The Supreme Personality of Godhead Himself is like an ocean of mercy. But, oh, no one can take water directly from the ocean. When you are in the land, in the desert, and you have no water, you cannot live, then who will save you? If the water from that ocean will come in the form like a cloud, and then rain water on you, you will be saved. So sad-guru is like this:
