Those who had been doing sādhana for hundreds of lifetimes, and now took birth in Kṛṣṇa's līlā, for them? No, not only for them. For all devotees, even in the future, for thousands of years to come. So Kṛṣṇa has done this līlā for what purpose? "Tu!" For you. To give mercy to you. "Yāḥ śrutvā tat-paro bhavet"(2), Śukadeva Gosvāmī said, and hearing this līlā, it is your duty to become absorbed in it. So this meaning, tat-para means absorbed. It does not only mean that you should become absorbed in hearing the līlā, but it means that by being absorbed in hearing the līlā, you will personally, individually, be absorbed into the eternal līlā yourself. So in the same way, we have kīrtana so that that transcendental, divine kathā will manifest here amongst us.
