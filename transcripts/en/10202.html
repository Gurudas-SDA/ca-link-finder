He said, "This is the essence of scriptures." When one, evaṁ-vrataḥ, has made a vow to chant a fixed number of Harināma every day, priya-nāma-kīrtyā, of the names which are very dear to him, like Rādhā-Ramaṇa, Rādhā-Vinoda-bihārī, (..) Rādhākānta, Gopīnātha, and Hare Kṛṣṇa, those names which are very dear to him, what happens? Evaṁ-vrataḥ sva-priya-nāma-kīrtyā jātānurāgo, he gets anurāga, deep eagerness, druta-citta, and his citta melts. Uccaiḥ hasaty, he laughs loudly, roditi, rauti, he cries and he calls out loudly, he dances like a mad person, loka-bāhyaḥ, and he doesn't care for external opinion of anyone around him or in society. Why? Because what happens has when the bhāva came, his citta melted, he can't even see what's going on, but he's having a vision of the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa. And if it's Kṛṣṇa leaving Vṛndāvana on Akrūra's chariot, he starts crying. If it's Kṛṣṇa dressing up as a monster and jumping out on in front of a gopī in the forest, "Grr!" And the gopī runs for her life and He throws off His costume and He runs around like this, and he's and He says, "Oh, don't be afraid. There's a hiding place here." And then He leads that gopī into a cave. (..)? He's the one who scares her, and then shows up like the hero who's come to save the day. He makes the problem and gives the solution at the same time. And then she's in the cave and she's like, "Oh, Kṛṣṇa, if you hadn't appeared here today, I would have been dead. I don't know what would have happened without you. How can I ever repay you?" Then Kṛṣṇa moves His eyebrows. You see? So, the devotee sees this and he's just laughing hysterically, he can't stop laughing. And he starts dancing like this. So, how is this all going on? It's because of the appearance of bhāva and it gives us it melts away our worldly perception and gives us the divine perception. So, that's what Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī is explaining here in his expanded explanation of what it really means, the appearance of bhāva-bhakti. Accha. Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmīpāda kī jaya!
