When Indra tried to destroy Vraja-maṇḍala with heavy rain, then Śrī Kṛṣṇa saved all the Vrajavāsīs by lifting Govardhana Hill. So Indra's plans were thwarted. And his later his guru told him, "Oh, you have made a big mistake. And I cannot help you. You should find out from Lord Brahmā what to do." So Lord Brahmā told him, "Oh, you should go to Govardhana with the mother of all cows, Surabhi, and take forgiveness from Śrī Kṛṣṇa, and perform abhiṣeka of Kṛṣṇa, bathing ceremony." So when Indra performed the bathing ceremony of Kṛṣṇa on the top of Govardhana Hill here, then the caraṇāmṛta ran down and made this kuṇḍa, Govinda-kuṇḍa. And Śrī Kṛṣṇa forgave Indra for his offenses.

[00:02:00] So this is a place where you can come, give praṇāma to Govinda-kuṇḍa, take water upon your head, and pray for forgiveness for all the offenses that we have committed.
