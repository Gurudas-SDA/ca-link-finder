And we will also try, (..) to recite and listen Śrī Śrīmad-Bhāgavata, serve Śrī Śrīmad-Bhāgavata, so that we can get prema. Bolo Śrīmad-Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa kī jaya! Jaya!
