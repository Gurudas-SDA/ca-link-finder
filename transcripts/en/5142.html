If Śrī Rūpa-mañjarī will not take a benediction, then Rādhikā will not be pleased. But bhakti is

We don't want any benedictions. So if the devotee will ask for the benediction, also the devotee will the heart will not be satisfied. So Rūpa Gosvāmī has such bhajana-cāturyā that he is asking for a benediction which includes that situation which will give the most happiness to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.
