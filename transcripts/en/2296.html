(1) The uniformity of the introduction and conclusion, (2) repetition, (3) originality (not known from other sources), (4) result or purpose, (5) praise (arthavāda), and (6) reasoning (upapatti)—these are the six signs of the purport. The meaning of a great sentence should also be understood by the general drift through positive and negative concomitance.
