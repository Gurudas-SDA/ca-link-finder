nirvāti śīghram athavā laghutām upaiti		By Gemini 2.5 Pro : "O friend, the love of two lovers is like a lamp; it shines steadily, illuminating the chamber of the heart. But if it is brought out through the door of the mouth, it is quickly extinguished or becomes dim."

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Rādhārāṇī Herself said that prema is like a flame of a lamp which is always burning in the cottage of the heart, and the light of that love, it comes out through the eyes, it shines from the face, but never comes out from the mouth. The mouth is like the door of the cottage: if you'll open the door of the cottage and bring that love outside by expressing it in words, then the wind of pride will make the love flicker, and perhaps even go out, it will reduce it. So love is never expressed, it's always hidden, but it shows in an indirect way. If love is directly expressed, then it will go down, it will be cheapened.

Just like one man, he had a friend, and the friend came to his house, and the friend saw, "Oh, he's cooking samosas and spinach and kheer, my favorite preparations!" So then he asked, "Oh, are you cooking for me?" His friend said, "No, I'm cooking for me." And then he cooked and offered, and then very silently he put them on the table before his friend. Then his friend is, "Oh, I know that he knows these are my favorite things and he cooked them for me," and his heart is melting, and he's eating, and the two of them inside, they relish so much love, but without words, no words. But if he said, "Did you cook this for me?" then he said, "Yes, I spent so many hours yesterday going to the supermarket, and the ingredients were so expensive, and I've been up since early this morning cooking," then the love will go down. But when the love is not expressed, when it's hidden, then it's relished heart to heart.

So in the same way, when Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa meet, then they never tell, "I love you," "Yes, I also love you." They'll say, "Oh, what are you doing here? You are a thief, you have to pay a tax," "I will not pay a tax, if the air that touches you touches my ghee, then it will be ruined!" So this is what? All prema-kalaha, the kathā of love. And by this quarrel, then only they are arranging for their [..]abhisāra, exactly where they will meet, when they will meet (..) for their next pastime the next day.

[00:23:48] So then the next day, Kṛṣṇa was waiting here, and Rādhikā and Her sakhīs came here, and see Kṛṣṇa took the tax (..) from Śrīmatī Rādhikā, that means He played with Her in the kuñjas here. But Rādhārāṇī took the tax back, why? Because the prema of Rādhikā was so high, that in their loving pastimes, Śrī Kṛṣṇa fainted, so She took the tax of Kṛṣṇa's citta. So the place where Rādhārāṇī took the tax back by making Kṛṣṇa faint in the ecstasy of prema, that is called Dāna-nivartana-kuṇḍa. So here is Dāna-ghāṭī, where Kṛṣṇa demanded the tax, and here is where Rādhikā took everything from Kṛṣṇa, She took His consciousness, made Him faint, so really Kṛṣṇa became (..) anaṅga, unconscious of His own aṅga, of His own body, here in Dāna-nivartana-kuṇḍa.

So Caitanya Mahāprabhu was the first person to manifest the greatness of the anurāga of Śrīmatī Rādhikā, and He empowered this realization in the heart of Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī. And he has expressed it in his Dāna-keli-kaumudī, Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī in his Dāna-keli-cintāmaṇi. This is the great treasure of the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas. This is why it is said:

sevāṁ vivekī na kaḥ		Due to Śrī Kṛṣṇa having taken birth there, the abode of Mathurā is superior even to Vaikuṇṭha, the realm of spiritual opulence. Superior to Mathurā is the forest of Vṛndāvana because there the festival of the rāsa dance took place. Superior to Vṛndāvana forest is Govardhana Hill because Śrī Kṛṣṇa raised it with His benedictive lotus hand and performed many pastimes there with His devotees. Yet superior even to Govardhana Hill is Śrī Rādhā-kuṇḍa because it is overflooded by the nectar of divine love felt by the Master of Gokula. What intelligent person would not desire to render service to this magnificent pond, which is gracefully situated at the base of Govardhana Hill?

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Mathurā is higher than Vaikuṇṭha, Vṛndāvana is higher than Mathurā, and Govardhana is higher than Vṛndāvana, why? Because the anurāga of Rādhikā was manifest in such a beautiful way here. So give praṇāma, touch the dust of this place, the dust of the lotus feet of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, and pray to Girirāja Govardhana:
