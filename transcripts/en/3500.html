Brahmā was utterly astonished seeing the liberation of Aghāsura by the Lord, a liberation unheard of for such a being in any other incarnation or from the source of incarnations. Desiring to see more of His wonderful glories, he took the calves and cowherd boys elsewhere and disappeared. Thus he saw His further glories, as is understood from the essence of this section. And this vision of further glory was not mere imagination. By verses starting "tāvat sarve vatsa-pālāḥ" [SB 10.13.46], it is said that Śrī Kṛṣṇa, by His partial expansions, manifested and showed directly to Brahmā millions of such lords of universes, each worshipped separately by their respective potencies, opulences, the twenty-four elements, time, nature, and the universes born from them, along with their creators like Brahmā and all living beings down to the blades of grass. [Bhāvārtha-dīpikā, 10.2.1] If he considered others also to be the supreme shelter, then specifying the "tenth" would be meaningless. Therefore, "Nārāyaṇa is Your limb" is well said.
