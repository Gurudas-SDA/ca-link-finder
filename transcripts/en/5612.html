Supreme Lord said that, "I am not independent, I am subordinate to the love of My devotees." So Kṛṣṇa said, "Oh, mākhana-corī chūṭe nā maiyā. Oh mother, I cannot stop stealing butter. Whatever will be, will be." Seeing Kṛṣṇa in this state and tears coming from His eyes, Mother Yaśodā took her Lālā in her lap and embraced Him. And she was in anxiety about the future.

[00:36:17] Just then, at that very moment, coming from a meeting with his counselors Upananda, Abhinanda, Sananda, and Nandana and other senior cowherd men in his parliament, Nandakirak, then Nanda Mahārāja arrived there. And he came, he has a very beautiful beard, which is black and white mixed together like a mixture of sesame and mustard seeds. So Nanda Bābā was very jolly and overjoyed to come back to see his son. So as soon as he entered the room, the whole room became bright with his jolly mood. He could see that Kṛṣṇa had been crying. So to cheer Him up, he called, "Oh Vatsa, come and sit in my lap." And Kṛṣṇa ran over to His father and jumped into the lap of His father, and Nanda Bābā embraced Him and sniffed His head. So Kṛṣṇa is very clever, He reached around the neck of His father and said, "Oh Bābā, why is Mā chastising me for nothing?" Nanda Bābā looked at Mother Yaśodā and said, "What is this all about, what's going on?" Kṛṣṇa said, "Oh mother, tell him the truth, what happened." Then Mother Yaśodā began to list off the whole series of complaints that had been made by all the neighbors. So Nanda Mahārāja is very wise. He had to decide and take a judgment what should be done. He looked at Kṛṣṇa and he looked at Mother Yaśodā, and then he announced his verdict. He said, "My son is as faultless as a valuable jewel. You know if a jewel has some fault in it, it's not so valuable, but if a jewel is free from all faults, then it's very valuable." So he said, "My son is like that, faultless as a valuable jewel. He has not done anything wrong at all." And then Kṛṣṇa looked at His father and was smiling with love, "Oh, my Bābā is so wonderful." And then Nanda Bābā said to Yaśodā, "And you, by siding with those persons who have made fun of Kṛṣṇa and who have made false accusations and have been envious towards Him, then by siding with them, you have wrongly accused my boy. And for this unfair behavior, you have to be punished." So Nanda Mahārāja was hiding his real mood with these words. This is the nature of Vraja. Kṛṣṇa is ṭehṛī-ṭehṛī, Kṛṣṇa is crooked, His Yamunā is crooked, the way He stands is crooked, the way He speaks is crooked, the way He glances is crooked, and His Vrajavāsīs are also crooked. (..) So Nanda Bābā said, "Oh, for taking the side of those who are envious against my son, so you'll have to be punished." Then he said, "Oh Lāṛalī Lāla, oh my darling son, come and sit in your lap and just stay with me and don't go anywhere else." But now Kṛṣṇa was startled because He thought, "Oh, my mother is going to be punished for My behavior." So immediately He jumped out of the lap of Nanda Mahārāja and ran and jumped into the lap of Mother Yaśodā. And then both Nanda Mahārāja and Mother Yaśodā, they burst out with hearty laughter.
