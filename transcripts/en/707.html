atha visrambhātiśayātmakaḥ premā praṇayaḥ, yasmin jāte sambhramādi-yogyatāyām api tad-abhāvaḥ | priyatvātiśayābhimānena kauṭilyābhāsa-pūrvaka-bhāva-vaicitrīṁ dadhat praṇayo mānaḥ, yasmin jāte śrī-bhagavān api tat-praṇaya-kopāt prema-mayaṁ bhayaṁ bhajate | ceto-dravātiśayātmakaḥ premaiva snehaḥ, yasmin jāte tat-sambandhābhāsenāpi mahā-bāṣpādi-vikāraḥ priya-darśanādy-atṛptis tasya parama-sāmarthyādau saty api keṣāṁcid aniṣṭāśaṅkā ca jāyate | sneha evābhilāṣātiśayātmako rāgaḥ, yasmin jāte kṣaṇikasyāpi virahasyātyantaivāsahiṣṇutā | tat-saṁyoge paraṁ duḥkham api sukhatvena bhāti, tad-viyoge tad-viparītam | sa eva rāgo’nukṣaṇaṁ sva-viṣayaṁ nava-navatvenānubhāvayan svayaṁ ca nava-navībhavann anurāgaḥ, yasmin jāte paraspara-vaśībhāvātiśayaḥ | prema-vaicittyaṁ tat-sambandhiny aprāṇiny api janma-lālasā, vipralambhe visphūrtiś ca jāyate | anurāga evāsamordhva-camatkāreṇonmādako mahā-bhāvaḥ | yasmin jāte yoge nimeṣāsahatā kalpa-kṣaṇatvam ity-ādikam | viyoge kṣaṇa-kalpatvam ity-ādikam | ubhayatra mahoddīptāśeṣa-sāttvika-vikārādikaṁ jāyate iti saṁskāra-hetavo guṇā darśitāḥ |Now, prema characterized by an abundance of confidence is praṇaya, and when it arises, there is an absence of formal respect, even when such formality would be appropriate. Praṇaya, which, through an esteemed sense of excessive dearness, exhibits a variety of moods preceded by apparent crookedness, is māna. When this arises, even the blessed Lord feels a love-filled fear from that display of loving anger. Prema itself, characterized by an excessive melting of the heart, is sneha. When this arises, even the semblance of a connection with Him causes great transformations like profuse tears, insatiability in seeing Him, and, despite His supreme power, apprehension of some misfortune for Him. Sneha itself, characterized by an abundance of longing, is rāga. When this arises, even a momentary separation becomes utterly unbearable. In union with Him, even great pain appears as happiness, and in separation from Him, the opposite occurs. That very rāga, causing one to experience its object as ever-new at every moment, and itself becoming ever-new, is anurāga. When this arises, there is an extreme mutual subjugation, prema-vaicittya (experiencing separation in union), a yearning to be born even as an inanimate object connected to Him, and vivid visions of Him in separation. Anurāga itself, which maddens one with a wonder that has no equal or superior, is mahā-bhāva. When it arises, in union, a moment's blink becomes unbearable and a kalpa feels like a moment, etc. In separation, a moment feels like a kalpa, etc. In both states, all the sāttvika transformations become fully and intensely manifest. Thus, the qualities that cause refinement (saṁskāra) have been shown.

Sri Kṛṣṇa said, "(..) I am the heart of My devotee, and the devotee is My heart. I don't know anything but My devotee, and My devotee doesn't know anything but Me." So in dāsya, sakhya and vātsalya, from the beginning of prema, the Lord is controlled by His devotee, and the devotee is controlled by his Lord. But not in mādhurya-rasa. Because (..) the prema in mādhurya-rasa has so many antagonistic elements. Internal, not external ones, internal. Like lajjā, shyness... Like vāmyatā, contrary mood... And māna... And asūyā, means hostility... All this in parakīya-bhāva, there are so many ways in which the heroine, she does not come under control of the hero at all. Even though prema is there. Even prema, sneha, māna, praṇaya, rāga, still the heroine is not under control of the love of the hero. Because of contrary mood. If Kṛṣṇa will approach Rādhārāṇī... She will say, "Oh, don't come near me. Even if the air that touches Your body will touch me, I'll be contaminated." Very strong contrary mood will come. But when the prema comes up to the stage of anurāga... Once, Rādhārāṇī was searching for Kṛṣṇa in the forest. Very desperately looking for Him everywhere. And Kṛṣṇa was desperately looking for Rādhārāṇī. And then by accident, by chance... They suddenly bumped into each other. And when They saw each other, at once They embraced very tight. What does it mean? Rādhikā is in the stage of anurāga. Because if prema was not intensified up to anurāga, then what will happen?

Vishvanatha Cakravarti Ṭhākura said, "After bathing and decorating Rādhārāṇī and bringing Her to meet with Kṛṣṇa, the moment Rādhārāṇī saw Kṛṣṇa coming, She turned around and began to walk the other way." So if the love of Rādhikā will increase up māna, praṇaya, rāga, up to anurāga... Then if She'll suddenly meet Kṛṣṇa by accident... And then heart melting and They embrace each other, this is called paraspara-vaśī... Paraspara means mutual. Vaśī-bhāva means controlled atiśaye. Vaśī means controlled. Bhāva means emotion, and atiśaye means excessive. So They've come under the control of each other's mutual love, it causes excessive amount of emotion. Vaśī is controlled. And bhāva is emotion, atiśaye is excessive. So a devotee hearing the pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, "Oh, that They meet and She walked away, or They meet and They embrace," they can feel of what level prema has increased to. Especially rasika.
