Śrī Vṛndā-devī kī jaya! Śrī Kāmyavana-parikramā kī jaya! Gaura-premānande haribol! Now quickly with kīrtana, we'll try to discover our bus. And from here we'll go and have breakfast at Bhojana-sthalī.
