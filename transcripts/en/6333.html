" Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī, it was nighttime, he was sitting on the bank of Rādhā-kuṇḍa. Now in those days, you know, when Mahāprabhu discovered Rādhā-kuṇḍa, (..) it was some rice paddy fields that were flooded, and the water was very shallow and muddy. But Mahāprabhu, in His divine vision, He realized this is Śyāma-kuṇḍa and Rādhā-kuṇḍa, but the local farmers just called it Gaurī-khet and Kālī-khet, the light field and the dark field. Anyway, Mahāprabhu, He took bath there in the shallow water, He took some mud and made a tilaka as well, and He discovered that place. So later, Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī came and he was doing bhajana there at night, and he was praying, "O Rādhā-kuṇḍa, (..) please give me your darśana, let me see." And he was chanting for hours through the night, and then he saw by the mercy of Śrīmatī Rādhikā, because Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Rādhikā are not two things, Rādhā-kuṇḍa is Rādhārāṇī's abhinna-svarūpa, it is another form of Rādhārāṇī. You see? And then he saw, "yadā tava sarovaraṁ sarasa-bhṛṅga-saṅghollasat"(3). At first he heard, a symphony of so many bumblebees, not one or two, but swarms of them, singing as if they're blowing on the conch of Kāmadeva to announce that (..) the love battle of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa will begin. And these bumblebees, they are so intoxicated because there are hundreds and thousands of different types of lotus flowers opening, blossoming in the kuṇḍa, and they're just wandering, the swarm is wandering from one lotus flower to another, to another, and they're becoming intoxicated, and they don't know where to go, they're blinded in ecstasy. And the water is so clear and fragrant and sparkling, so attractive, and around the kuṇḍa, there are tamāla trees, there are kadamba trees, and mālatī, belī, camelī, and so many different flowers, and all the fragrance combined together, the fragrance of all the flowers combined together is just like the fragrance of Rādhikā. The lotus flowers are like the lotus eyes of Rādhikā. The swans swimming in the lake, they're singing, it sounds like the ankle bells of Rādhikā. The breeze is blowing, and it's like the sighs of Rādhikā. This Rādhā-kuṇḍa is so astonishingly beautiful. And there, Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī had the darśana of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa and the sakhīs. They were covered in colors because they'd just been playing Holī-līlā. So now, they'd finished that līlā and they decided to play in the water. And Kṛṣṇa and Rādhikā and the sakhīs entered into the water, and as they entered into the water, then the colors which were all over their bodies melted and began to spread in the water. And they were there playing. And so many sakhīs and Rādhikā, they all surrounded Kṛṣṇa, and they were splashing Him on all sides. So Kṛṣṇa thought, "This is not fair." So He dove under the water, and He swam very far away under the water and He came up. They're like, "Where is He? Where did He go?" And then they saw, "Ah, He's there." Kṛṣṇa said, "This is not fair, I will only play this game one by one. So first, I'll play with Her," pointing to Rādhikā. So then Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa came together and they began to splash each other. And Kṛṣṇa is very physical, so He splashed the water so powerfully in the eyes of Rādhā, and Rādhārāṇī was rubbing Her eyes, "Oh, I can't see, I can't see." And Kṛṣṇa was laughing. But Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī is so overwhelmed with greed for Rādhikā's service that he cannot tolerate that Kṛṣṇa had splashed water in Rādhikā's eyes. His svarūpa as Rati Mañjarī manifested, and Rati Mañjarī immediately jumped into the water right next to Rādhikā to defend Her and splash Kṛṣṇa in the face. This is sevā. Rādhā-sevā. And just as she was splashing Kṛṣṇa in the face, then suddenly, everything disappeared. And Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī found himself sitting in the flooded rice field at night, alone, crying, "Hā Rādhe! Hā Rādhe!" "Tad-arthe tavaiva mama lālasājani tavaiva dāsya-rase, O Rādhikā, since I beheld the beauty of Your kuṇḍa filled with humming rasika bumblebees, lotus flowers, and beautiful water, so many trees and fragrances, so since that moment, I really developed a greed to become Your dāsī."

[01:31:33] This is the whole purpose of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. This is why we want to give up nāma-aparādha, because aparādha means against Rādhā, against Rādhā-dāsyam. So one should try to avoid these things, and on the other hand, try to be in the (^)association of very high-class rasika Vaiṣṇavas, and hear beautiful hari-kathā, and then some vision may come, you know, the saṁskāras will come and gradually develop, intense greed will develop, and that greed will impel us towards our perfection in life. Vṛndāvana-dhāma kī jaya! Rādhā-sarovara kī jaya! Jaya Jaya Śrī Rādhe-e-e Śyām! Gaura-premānande Hari Haribol! So let us engage in the pūrṇa, the aṅgī of bhakti, complete and perfect bhakti, Śrī Harināma-saṅkīrtana.
