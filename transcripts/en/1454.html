"O Rādhārāṇī, if You want to give mercy to me, I ask nothing for myself, I only want that mercy that if Kṛṣṇa is going to meet with You, before He meets with You, He'll have to take permission from me first." When Rādhikā heard this prayer of Rūpa Gosvāmī, She was very happy. So that mood is expressed. Boliye Vṛndāvana-vihārī-lāla kī jaya! Jaya! Barsāne-vālī kī jaya! Jaya! Jaya Jaya Śrī Rādhe!
