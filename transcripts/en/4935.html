May all living entities become calm by practicing bhakti-yoga, for by accepting devotional service they will think of each other's welfare. It means, "svasty astu viśvasya"(1) - may everyone in the universe become auspicious. And the wicked people, they should also be happy. Everyone should be happy. How can they be happy? "Dhyāyantu bhūtāni śivaṁ mitho dhiyā"(1) - the people of the world can only be happy when they meditate on each other's happiness. Instead of thinking of their own happiness, they think, "Oh, how can my brother be happy? How can my sister be happy?" This is the problem, everyone is saying, "How can I be happy?" and everyone is in conflict. If everyone is only thinking of others, then everyone will be happy. But who can think only of the welfare of others? "Manaś ca bhadraṁ bhajatām adhokṣaje"(1) - only those whose hearts are pure. If the heart is not pure, then you must be self-absorbed. So how can the heart be pure? "Āveśyatāṁ no matir apy ahaitukī"(1) - by always meditating on Śrī Kṛṣṇa and serving Him without any conditions. So this is the wisdom of Prahlāda Mahārāja. Only those who are absorbed in Kṛṣṇa can have a pure heart, only those who have a pure heart can think of the welfare of others, and only those who think of the welfare of others can bring happiness to the whole world.
