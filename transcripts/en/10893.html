So we pray to Ramesh Bābā that though I am blind and I cannot see you, I know that you are always here, and I pray that again and again, again and again, I may always come and take the dust of your lotus feet. Nitya-līlā-praviṣṭa oṁ viṣṇupāda aṣṭottara-śata Śrī Ramesh Bābājī Mahārāja kī jaya! Everyone please give praṇāma, leave some praṇāmī for the service of Śrī Śrī Rādhā Māna-bihārī. And we'll continue on parikramā to the temple of Śrī Śrī Rādhā Kusuma-bihārī.
