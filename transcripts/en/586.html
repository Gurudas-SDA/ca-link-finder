So Rādhārāṇī was feeling regret, that She had engaged Kṛṣṇa in Her service in this way. So then She cried, "Dāsyās te kṛpaṇāyā me! Oh, just be merciful to Your—I am Your dāsī, I am Your servant, Your maidservant. I'm Your wretched maidservant. But (..) You had served Me in so many ways and made Me feel so fortunate, I became intoxicated with My fortune. And I was so tired from the very energetic pastimes with You, that I became tired and sleepy, and I spoke the wrong words." Then She said, "Sakhe, oh, friend! You treated Me like such an intimate friend, so I was treating You like an intimate friend. (..) So please come back." Then Kṛṣṇa said, "I'm here right in front of you." Rādhārāṇī said, "Darśaya sannidhim, I am so blinded by lamentation I can't see You, take Me to where You are." And then She fainted and fell to the ground unconscious.
