[00:41:29] Śrī Kṛṣṇa has left Vṛndāvana on the chariot of Akrūra and gone to Mathurā. And Rādhikā is weeping. She said, "Ayi dīna-dayārdra nātha he(1)", "Oh Nātha, You are my Gopīnātha." You are the—that "Nātha" means "prāṇa"—"You are the prāṇa, the life and soul, my life and soul and the life and soul of gopīs of Vṛndāvana. Dīna-dayārdra-nātha, and Your heart is always melting in compassion for those persons who are suffering." So in the ocean of Rādhārāṇī's prema, when She says this first line, then the waves, sañcārī-bhāvas are coming. First, nirveda. Nirveda means ātma-nindā. That is, Rādhārāṇī is criticizing Herself, "Oh, I am most wretched. I have no love at all. Because my Śyāmasundara has gone and I'm still breathing. That means I must have no love." Then next wave of emotion, fighting with the first, that is called the viṣāda. Viṣāda means anutāpa, regret, "I had so many dreams and so many desires, so many services I wanted to render to Kṛṣṇa. I wanted to please Him in so many ways, but now He's gone, alas, why did not I (..) do these things before?" Then dainya, humility, a wave of humility came. Humility means ātmani ayogya-buddhi, having the idea that I am completely unqualified. So just then, another wave of emotion came, asūyā, hostility. Hostility means when a person has all good qualities, but you looking for some fault in them. The next moment Rādhikā has become angry with Kṛṣṇa,
