Brahmā was utterly astonished seeing the liberation of Aghāsura by the Lord, a liberation unheard of for such a being in any other incarnation or from the source of incarnations. [Bhāvārtha-dīpikā, 10.2.1] If he considered others also to be the supreme shelter, then specifying the "tenth" would be meaningless. Therefore, "Nārāyaṇa is Your limb" is well said.
