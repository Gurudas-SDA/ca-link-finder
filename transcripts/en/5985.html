Śrī Caitanya-Caritāmṛta (1975) by A. C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda

───────────────────────── [21]

anayārādhito nūnaṁ
bhagavān harir īśvaraḥ
yan no vihāya govindaḥ
prīto yām anayad rahaḥ		"Truly the Personality of Godhead has been worshiped by Her. Therefore Lord Govinda, being pleased, has brought Her to a lonely spot, leaving us all behind."

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

How in the rāsa-līlā, Śrī Kṛṣṇa disappeared, leaving all Vraja-gopīs, only He went away with Śrīmatī Rādhikā. And then the gopīs saw Kṛṣṇa's footprints and saw Rādhikā's footprints next to Kṛṣṇa and said, "Oh, this gopī must have worshipped Bhagavān Hari Īśvara more than anyone else, because her desire to be with the cowherd boy Govinda has been fulfilled; Govinda has taken her away with Himself alone, leaving all of us." So in Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, this verse is like the beating of kettle-drums, announcing the supremacy of Śrīmatī Rādhikā throughout the entire universe. Jaya Jaya Śrī Rādhe!

tasmiṁs tuṣṭe jagat tuṣṭaṁ
prīyatāṁ puṇḍarīkākṣaḥ sarva-yajñeśvaro hariḥ │
tasmiṁs tuṣṭe jagat tuṣṭaṁ prīṇite prīṇitaṁ bhavet ||
“May Hari of lotus eyes, the Lord of all sacrifices (yajñās), be pleased. When he is satisfied, the world is satisfied, and when he is pleased, the world is pleased.”

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

, when Kṛṣṇa is pleased, the whole universe becomes satisfied. So because Śrīla Vyāsadeva, in his final contribution, Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, he has presented the supreme glories of Śrīmatī Rādhikā, therefore Kṛṣṇa was satisfied, and when Kṛṣṇa was satisfied, then finally Śrīla Vyāsadeva become fully satisfied. So this is the message of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam: try to make the goal of your life rādhā-dāsyam, to become the maidservant of Śrīmatī Rādhikā. Gaura-premānande, Haribol!
