And in the Śikṣāṣṭakam, step by step, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu has told for everyone at every level of adhikāra, how to go through each stage of progress so that the holy name will come and dance on one's tongue, along with rasa, prema-rasa, and therefore it's called Śikṣāṣṭakam. gaura-premānande hari hari bol!
