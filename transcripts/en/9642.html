Oh, what a mistake. It could happen to anyone." Then Krishna and all Vrajavāsīs become ecstatic by this teasing.

So Vidyāpati, he has written one beautiful kīrtana.

The meaning is Rādhikā said to her sakhī, "Ki kahiba re sakhi, āju kī ānanda ora(21). O sakhī, what can I say to you today about my ānanda, my joy, because after a long time now Mādhava, Krishna is here in my kuñja-mandira." So this song expresses Rādhārāṇī's joy when Krishna came back to Braj. Then Rādhikā turned to Krishna and said, "Aneka dine tumi morāi... ghare te pāyāchi ebe, rākhiba(21), for many days you cheated me by staying far away. But now I have got you in my kuñja-mandira, I will tie you up and I will never let you go." So this is why Caitanya Mahāprabhu told the Rāmānanda Vasu, "You have to bring some stronger ropes."
