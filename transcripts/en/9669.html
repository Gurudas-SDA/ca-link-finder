Brahmā was utterly astonished seeing the liberation of Aghāsura by the Lord, a liberation unheard of for such a being in any other incarnation or from the source of incarnations. By verses starting "tāvat sarve vatsa-pālāḥ" [SB 10.13.46], it is said that Śrī Kṛṣṇa, by His partial expansions, manifested and showed directly to Brahmā millions of such lords of universes, each worshipped separately by their respective potencies, opulences, the twenty-four elements, time, nature, and the universes born from them, along with their creators like Brahmā and all living beings down to the blades of grass. [Bhāvārtha-dīpikā, 10.2.1] If he considered others also to be the supreme shelter, then specifying the "tenth" would be meaningless. Therefore, "Nārāyaṇa is Your limb" is well said.
