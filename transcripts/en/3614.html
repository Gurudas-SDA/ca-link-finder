The love of Rādhikā is My dancing teacher, and I am the pupil. Her love makes Me dance every day no more new and new, novel dances. So there are two Dāmodaras: Yaśodā-Dāmodara and Rādhā-Dāmodara. So that Dāmodara who is controlled by the love of Rādhikā is Rādhā-Dāmodara.
