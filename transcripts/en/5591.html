tad vṛndāvana-nāmni dhāmni parame svīyaṁ vapur nāśyatu		“The happiness tasted by those whose eyes are immersed in the ocean of loving pastimes in Rādhā’s town—compared with that, all the festivals of happiness enjoyed by the Supreme Lord amount not even to the tiniest fraction. If anyone, by incomparable good fortune, has obtained even a hope for that joy, then let his own body perish in that supreme abode known as Vṛndāvana.”

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

The meaning is this, that all the ānanda which is experienced by Kṛṣṇa in all of His pastimes, in Vaikuṇṭha, in Mathurā, Dvārakā, in Ayodhyā, in all dhāmas, and also all the bliss experienced by Śrī Kṛṣṇa in His pastimes in Vṛndāvana with His devotees, that cannot be compared, that itself is even only cannot be compared to an atom of the bliss that the sakhīs, the mañjarīs of Rādhikā experience when with their eyes they see the meeting of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

[01:42:22] So you can argue, "Oh, but Kṛṣṇa is raso vai saḥ. Kṛṣṇa is ānandamayo ’bhyāsāt. He is sat-cit-ānanda. He is made of bliss." But Kṛṣṇa has not realized the bliss of Śrīmatī Rādhikā. But the maidservants of Rādhikā, they become completely permeated with Rādhikā’s mādanākhya-mahābhāva. So, "no tal-leśa-lavāyati bhagavataḥ sarvāpi saukhyotsavaḥ." Of all the festivals of bliss that Kṛṣṇa experiences, even that cannot be compared to one drop of the bliss of the eyes of the maidservants of Śrīmatī Rādhikā watching the Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa yugala-milana, divine meeting.
