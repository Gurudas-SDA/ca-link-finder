So, Kṛṣṇa said to Arjuna, He said, "Look, everyone's dead already, and all I'm asking you to do is just become an instrument of My will. So, then, seeing this, Arjuna became relieved of his ego, his ahaṅkāra. Because before he was thinking, "I am the doer. Shall I fight or shall I not fight? I am doing." But when he saw the universal form, he realized that Kṛṣṇa's doing everything. "I was in illusion." All things everywhere are nothing but Kṛṣṇa's energy, and Kṛṣṇa's doing everything. Because if He had told him, he would not understand, because he had too much ego. But after his ego had been relieved, seeing, "Wow, I thought I was doing everything, now I understand I'm not doing anything at all. Kṛṣṇa is doing everything." So, being free from ego, then when Kṛṣṇa said to surrender, he understood what it meant.
