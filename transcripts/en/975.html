Kṛṣṇa, like a river, entered into the ocean of Rādhā-bhāva and forgot His own name and form. He forgot that His name was Śyāmasundara, and He became Gaurasundara.

[00:57:22] Jaya Śacīnandana Gaura-hari! Jaya! So, with these words, Śrī Kṛṣṇa completed His pastimes in this world with an unfinished desire. And then, shortly afterwards, He appeared from the womb of Śacī-mātā on Gaura-pūrṇimā, and we will celebrate that in a few days. So, everywhere in Bhāgavatam, the glories of Rādhikā are hidden. And even the glories of Gaurasundara are there for those gaura-priya-jana who are qualified to see it. Premānande! Hari Hari bol!
