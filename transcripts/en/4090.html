Every word Rūpa Goswāmī has written is realized, he realized it first, then he wrote it. (..)? So stotraṁ yatra taṭasthatāṁ. You are crooked, you are hard, and restless." (..) These are faults in a person's character. To be restless, to be kauṭilya, to be crooked, and to be the kaṭhora, harsh. Rādhārāṇī just laughed.
