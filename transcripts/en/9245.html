Bhagavad-gītā As It Is by A. C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda

───────────────────────── [13]

ye yathā māṁ prapadyante
tāṁs tathaiva bhajāmy aham
mama vartmānuvartante
manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ		As all surrender unto Me, I reward them accordingly. Everyone follows My path in all respects, O son of Pṛthā.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

So bhakti is so glorious. And karma, jñāna, yoga, all useless, lame, cannot produce any eternal, permanent result. Even the desire to engage in these things is coming from the rajas. And the desire for jñāna, it may be sāttvika, but sattva is also māyā. The living entities in this world are bound by māyā. So if you're in a prison, it does not matter whether you are in the iron shackles, silver shackles, or golden shackles. So whether you are bound by tamo-guṇa, rajo-guṇa, or sattva-guṇa, it's still binding. So this pastime is like a kettledrum loudly proclaiming the glories of the bhakta, the Vaiṣṇava, over all worldly positions. Śrī Bhakti Devī kī jaya! Gaura-premānande! Haribol!
