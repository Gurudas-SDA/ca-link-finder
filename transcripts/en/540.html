mat-sandeśair vimocaya		[Lord Kṛṣṇa said:] Dear gentle Uddhava, go to Vraja and give pleasure to Our parents. And also relieve the gopīs, suffering in separation from Me, by giving them My message.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

This is a very important verse, if you learn Bhāgavatam verses, learn this one. Kṛṣṇa's deep attachment to the Vrajavāsīs is expressed here. The first word in any verse has emphasis. So, what is the first word? Gaccha. "Go! Go, Uddhava!" Where? Gacchoddhava vrajam. "Go to Vraja." Gacchoddhava vrajaṁ saumya. "Saumya means one whose face is like the moon. If your mind has any anxiety, and you see the full moon, then you feel peaceful. So, Uddhava had this very pleasant face. If anyone will see his face in their anxiety, then their anxiety will go away. So, Kṛṣṇa said, "Gacchoddhava vrajaṁ saumya, go to Vraja with your moon-like face, and may the rays of your moon-like face dispel, to some degree, the anxieties of the Vrajavāsīs." Pitror nau prītim āvaha, "And bring some happiness to our parents." It's very significant that Kṛṣṇa is saying, "To our parents." Actually, Nanda and Yaśodā are not Uddhava's parents. But Uddhava feels so much oneness with Kṛṣṇa. So, Kṛṣṇa is trying to impress upon him, "Oh, My parents, they are like your parents. Think how much anxiety they are in, and then you'll be very eager to go and pacify them." Pitror nau prītim āvaha gopīnāṁ mad-viyogādhim. "The gopīs are feeling viyoga, such separation from Me. So, mat-sandeśair vimocaya, vimocaya, completely liberate them from the pain of separation by delivering mat-sandeśa, My messages." This verse has so many profound meanings. (..) First of all, we see that Kṛṣṇa is asking Uddhava to pacify His parents in vātsalya-rasa and gopīs in mādhurya-rasa, He has not mentioned anything about friends in sakhya-rasa. Kṛṣṇa also said, "Bring happiness to My parents, Uddhava, but liberate the gopīs from the pain of their separation by giving My messages," means, "Uddhava, you are very intelligent. When you meet our parents, Nanda and Yaśodā, by your intelligence, see, you can speak in a very sweet and diplomatic way and pacify them. But when you see the gopīs, don't use your intelligence. Just deliver My message, what I am telling you, just repeat it exactly as I am telling you. Because you may be able to pacify our parents, but you have no idea what you're dealing (..) with the gopīs. So, don't even think about creating your own method of pacification, just repeat what I am telling you." The cowherd boys have not been mentioned. Because Kṛṣṇa is thinking, "Uddhava, don't say anything to them." Because their love is so intense that, in separation from Kṛṣṇa, they wake up in the morning and they're like mad. You see, "Oh, I am going to go and take the cows to graze with Kṛṣṇa." And they go out from their houses, and in the madness of their separation, they have sphūrti and they see Kṛṣṇa. And they become happy and they go. They're wandering around the forest, singing, dancing, thinking that Kṛṣṇa is there, but He's not there. "If you see the cowherd boys, shh, don't say anything. Because if you tell them, 'Oh, I have come from Mathurā with a message from Kṛṣṇa. He wants to pacify your separation,' then they will realize that I am not there. So, better not to say anything, and let them go on thinking in their experience that I am with them." So, cowherd boys' prema is called viśrambha-prema. And in this kind of love, if they have a sphūrti, a vision of Kṛṣṇa, they believe, "This is Kṛṣṇa. I am with Kṛṣṇa." They have faith in it. And it's true, this sphūrti is Kṛṣṇa. If any devotee will feel separation, they may have a glimpse of Kṛṣṇa in their separation, and really this is Kṛṣṇa. So, Kṛṣṇa has not mentioned to Uddhava, "Go and speak to the cowherd boys." He doesn't want Uddhava to speak to them. Let them be happy in the madness of their separation. But the parents and Vraja-gopīs, they don't have viśrambha-prema, they have utkaṇṭhāmayī-prema. It is a love which is imbued with a intense eagerness. This supernatural love is so astonishing that when they are in the fire of separation, they have sphūrti and they see Kṛṣṇa, but they don't believe that this is real. But even more than this— Especially for Vraja-gopīs— When they are actually with Kṛṣṇa, they feel separation at the time of meeting, and think, "I am not really with Kṛṣṇa. I think this is a sphūrti." So, they weep in loving separation even when they are with Him. Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī has written one poem, the Bṛhad-dhyāna, the great meditation. Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are sitting together in the middle of a beautiful lotus flower. And Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are looking into each other's eyes.

[01:53:47] And they are overwhelmed with sāttvika-bhāvas. And Rādhikā's tears are flowing that so many sakhīs, their complete time is taken only wiping up Her tears. Because Rādhikā's love is so intense that sometimes, even in meeting, She thinks that Kṛṣṇa is not there and this real Kṛṣṇa is just sphūrti. So, at this point, Uddhava, (..) he has no idea at all that there is such a love like the love of Vraja-gopīs. So, Kṛṣṇa said, "Pitror nau prītim āvaha, try to console My parents, but with gopīs, don't try anything. Just repeat My exact messages." Uddhava is very attached to Kṛṣṇa's association, he doesn't want to leave Him. So, Śrī Kṛṣṇa will have to persuade him somehow to give up His association and go there to Vraja. To convince him that, "Hey, Uddhava, I am only partially present here in Mathurā. If you will go there, you'll see there I am fully present. So really, by staying here, you are giving Me up. By going there, you are really staying with Me." It is very difficult to explain to someone who has never been in Vraja-līlā what it is like. But how Kṛṣṇa will try to explain this to Uddhava to inspire him to go there, we'll explain tomorrow.
