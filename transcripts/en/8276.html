[00:00:21] Śrī Prema Sarovara. One day Vṛṣabhānu-nandinī and Śyāmasundara, Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, They were sitting together here, surrounded by Their sakhīs, and engaged in the rasa-ālāpa, beautiful discussions of the mellows of love. At that time, because Rādhikā’s face is beautiful like a lotus flower, and because Her breath has the fragrance like a lotus flower, so a bumblebee became confused and was trying to land on the lotus face of Rādhikā to taste honey. With Her delicate fingers, like campaka buds, Rādhikā was trying to shoo away the bee. But She did—She was not successful; the bee kept approaching Her again and again, and She was quite afraid. So Madhumaṅgala, he decided to help, and he took his stick, and he chased the bumblebee far away. Then Madhumaṅgala came back and proudly declared, "Madhusūdana has gone far away, and he will not return." Madhusūdana means bumblebee, but also means Śrī Kṛṣṇa, who tastes the madhu, the prema of Vraja-gopīs. But Rādhikā, She heard this sound as if Madhumaṅgala was saying, "Kṛṣṇa has gone, and He will not return." At once, She began to cry.
