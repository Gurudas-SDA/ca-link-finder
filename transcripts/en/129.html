So knowledge, by knowledge we can protect ourselves, and by power we can protect ourselves. So we consider that knowledge and power are very important. So then we consider God must be omniscient, all-knowing, and all-powerful.

In the spiritual world, in the transcendental plane, beyond time, beyond space, there is no death, there is no danger, there is no hostile environment. So it must be concluded that in the transcendental plane, the only true values, the highest values are beauty and love.

Every single person in this world is attracted by beauty, and no one can feel fulfilled without discovering love. So ultimately, each and every person is searching for that ocean of beauty and love, Śrī Kṛṣṇa.
