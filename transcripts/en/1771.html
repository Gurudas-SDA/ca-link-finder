───────────────────────── [17]

bhāvanāyāḥ pade yas tu budhenānanya-buddhinā | bhāvyate gāḍha-saṃskāraiś citte bhāvaḥ sa kathyate ||2.5.133||“That which the wise person who has dedicated his intelligence only to the Lord experiences in his heart, which realizes vibhāva and the other elements as separate entities, through deep impressions of previous bhakti , is called bhāva .”

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

This stage of bhakti, wherein the devotee is meditating with ananya-buddhi, one-pointed attention, then on the basis of gāḍha-saṁskāra, deep impressions, he can perceive the sthāyibhāva, vibhāva, anubhāva, sāttvika-bhāva, vyabhicāri-bhāva, the ingredients of rasa. But still no experience of rasa. So

──── VERSE NOT FOUND IN THE DATABASE ────

vyatītya bhāvanā-vartma yaś camatkāra-bhāra-bhūḥ

hṛtte sa-camatkāre bhāvan raso svādyate mataḥ

───────────────────────── [18]

So Rūpa Gosvāmī said, "Oh, when one goes beyond vyatītya bhāvanā-vartma, beyond meditation, when the heart becomes flooded with a greater influx of viśuddha-sattva, then it is not possible to, experience the individual ingredients of rasa. But they all mix together and make an astonishing taste, and (..) that is called rasa." So how to go from bhāvamayī-bhakti to rasamayī-bhakti, this prayojana, that has been shown in the poetry of Raghunātha Dāsa Gosvāmī. Therefore, he is prayojana-tattva-ācārya. So that is somewhere down the line in the future for us. But to hear something that can leave so that one day we can, understand what is the glory of his contribution.
