yasya syur vīta-saṅkalpāḥ
prāṇendriya-mano-dhiyām
vṛttayaḥ sa vinirmukto
deha-stho ’pi hi tad-guṇaiḥ		A person is considered to be completely liberated from the gross and subtle material bodies when all the functions of his vital energy, senses, mind and intelligence are performed without material desire. Such a person, although situated within the body, is not entangled.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Aur conclusion meṅ ye kahā ki jisne apne saṅkalpoṅ kā tyāga kar diyā hai, the person who has leave left his whole desires, aur prāṇa, indriya, mana kī vṛtti inko jisne vaśa meṅ kar liyā hai, the subject of prāṇa, mana, and indriya, he is controlled his prāṇa, mana, and indriya, and he is not involving in the subjects, vinirmuktaḥ, unse jo mukta ho rahā hai, dehastho ’pi vinirmukto dehastho ’pi tat-guṇaiḥ. Vo śarīra meṅ rahate hue bhī śarīra ke guṇoṅ se mukta rahegā. Vahī vyākhyā jo cal rahī hai, vahī "Dvā suparṇā sayujā sakhāyā," isī kī vyākhyā yahāṅ par cal rahī hai ki do pakṣī haiṅ ek vṛkṣa meṅ rah rahe haiṅ, ek phala ko khā rahā hai, dūsrā phala ko nahīṅ khātā.
